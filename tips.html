Tech Tips

Simple guides and tricks to help you get the most out of your devices.

3 quick tips to declutter your email inbox

  • Use the search bar to find newsletters you don't read, then unsubscribe.
  • Create folders for categories like "Bills," "Family," and "Work."
  • Archive old emails instead of deleting them. You can still search for them later.

How to back up your photos safely

For the best protection, use cloud storage (Google Photos or iCloud) together with an external hard drive.

Make your phone easier to use

Increase the text size, turn on "Do Not Disturb" at night, and learn a few quick gestures to save time.
