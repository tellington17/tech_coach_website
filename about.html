About The Tech Guy

Helping people make sense of technology is my passion. With years of experience in software development and hands-on tech support, I’ve built a service around simplifying technology for everyday users and businesses alike.

Who I Am

I’m a technology professional with a background in software development and consulting. Outside of work, I’ve become the go-to “tech guy” for family and friends — helping them troubleshoot issues, set up new devices & systems, and feel confident using technology.

That same approachable, practical help is what inspired me to start this service — giving individuals and households access to clear, friendly, and reliable tech support without the confusion or jargon.

My Mission

To make technology easier to use, so you can get more out of the devices you rely on every day.
